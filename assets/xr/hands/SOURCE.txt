LeftHand.glb and RightHand.glb are packed from assets/gltf/LeftHandHumanoid.gltf
and RightHandHumanoid.gltf, their .bin buffers and the shared hand.png texture.
Mesh, weights, texture, joint hierarchy and rest transforms are preserved.
Godot humanoid joint axes are converted to OpenXR at runtime by XRHandPose.
Reproduce with: python tools/import_xr_hands.py
